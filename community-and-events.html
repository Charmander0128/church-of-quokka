Community and Events

Current status: This is an early-stage public creative project. No membership total or event attendance is claimed. The certificate on the home page is generated in the visitor’s browser and is not a registry of personal information.

How to participate honestly

  • Create an original leaf, quokka or “smile into the abyss” artwork.
  • Write a short fictional parable and label it as Church of Quokka worldbuilding.
  • Try one act of kindness or deliberate rest, without treating either as a performance.
  • Suggest corrections when a page could be mistaken for history or living cultural tradition.

Open creative activity: Seven Leaves Week

9–15 November 2026 · online and self-directed. Visitors are invited to complete seven small actions over seven days: rest, notice nature, help someone, make something, learn one verified fact, leave wildlife undisturbed and laugh at one harmless absurdity.

Propose a contribution

Artwork, fictional stories, corrections and event proposals can be suggested through the project’s public GitHub repository. Contributors should only submit work they created or have permission to share, and must label fiction clearly.

Event standard

If a gathering is announced, its page should state the organiser, date, location or platform, accessibility information, whether registration is required, and afterward what verifiably occurred. Proposed events remain labelled as proposals until confirmed.
