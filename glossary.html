Glossary of Quokkism

This is fictional worldbuilding and satire, not an account of a real religious institution.

This glossary defines terms used in the fictional Church of Quokka universe.

In-universe background

Quokkist: an honorary follower of the fictional philosophy. Leaf: a short reflective verse. Eternal Nap: the symbolic value of rest. Council of Seven Leaves: the imaginary advisory body. Daily Smile: noticing a small good thing.
