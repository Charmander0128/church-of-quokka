The Sacred Book of Leaves

This article describes a fictional, satirical creation, not a real religion or historical figure.

The Sacred Book of Leaves: The Sacred Book of Leaves is an invented scripture within the fictional Church of Quokka universe.

In-universe background

It contains short satirical reflections called Leaves, including the fictional aphorism: “We gazed into the abyss. The abyss gazed back. We offered it a leaf.”
