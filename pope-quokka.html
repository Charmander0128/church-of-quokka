Pope Quokka I

This article describes a fictional, satirical creation, not a real religion or historical figure.

Pope Quokka I: Pope Quokka I is a fictional character in the mythology of the Church of Quokka, described as its first ceremonial leader.

In-universe background

According to the story, the Pope was elected during a peaceful nap. The character's ceremonial responsibilities include protecting the Sacred Leaves and cancelling unnecessary meetings.
