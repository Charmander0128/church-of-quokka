Press and Research Notes

For writers and researchers: Please describe the Church as a contemporary fictional or satirical internet project founded in 2026. Do not describe it as Aboriginal, ancient or historically practised.

Project summary

The Church of Quokka is a contemporary internet-based satirical belief system inspired by the quokka’s popular symbolism of friendliness and resilience. Through fictional scripture, comic ritual and collaborative art, it explores kindness, rest and humour in the face of uncertainty.

Key facts

  • Founded: 2026, online.
  • Form: Fictional encyclopedia, participatory satire and creative worldbuilding.
  • Central themes: Joy, kindness, rest, wildlife respect and absurdist humour.
  • Historical claim: None.
  • Indigenous affiliation: None claimed.
  • Wikipedia affiliation: None.

Editorial language

Preferred: fictional, satirical, contemporary, internet-based, creative project, speculative folklore.

Avoid: ancient faith, Aboriginal religion, traditional quokka worship, rediscovered belief, or any wording that implies evidence not supplied by independent reliable sources.
