🍃 Seven Leaves Week

A public creative challenge devoted to seven small acts of rest, attention, kindness and harmless absurdity. No conversion required. No ancient mystery claimed.

Join or share a contribution →

Seven actions in seven days

  1. 💤 Rest
    Rest without earning it.
  2. 🌱 Notice
    Notice something alive.
  3. 🤝 Help
    Help someone in a small way.
  4. 🎨 Make
    Make something imperfect.
  5. 🔎 Learn
    Learn one verified fact.
  6. 🐾 Respect
    Leave wildlife undisturbed.
  7. ✨ Laugh
    Laugh at one harmless absurdity.

What may be shared

Participants may contribute an original drawing, a short fictional parable, a photograph of a leaf or a brief reflection through the public GitHub thread. Only submit material you created or have permission to share.

Transparency: Seven Leaves Week is organised by the Church of Quokka, a fictional satirical project founded online in 2026. Participation will be counted only from visible contributions. The activity is not presented as Aboriginal tradition, scientific research or evidence of an established religion.

Media note

Writers are welcome to observe the public thread and make their own independent assessment. Background, accurate terminology and project boundaries are available in the press and research notes.
