Welcome, curious creature

The Church of Quokka is a fictional, satirical philosophy inspired by the Australian quokka. In this imaginary universe, joy is a small rebellion against chaos, kindness is a sacred duty, and rest is never wasted.

Origins of Quokkism

Legend says the first Quokkist asked: “If the universe is indifferent, why does the quokka smile?” No answer came. A leaf fell. The Quokkist rested beneath a tree, and the Church was born—at least according to its entirely invented history.

The Quokka Pope

👑🐾

His Fluffiness, Pope Quokka I

The fictional first pontiff was elected by unanimous silence during an especially peaceful nap. His official duties include protecting the sacred leaf supply and refusing unnecessary meetings.

Official decree: “Be kind. Take naps. Do not eat the constitution.”

The Holy Council of Quokkas

In Church lore, the Council of Seven Leaves interprets the ancient teachings. Council sessions end immediately if any member falls asleep. Decisions are made by consensus, or by whichever leaf blows closest to the door.

The Five Commandments

  1. Smile at the absurdity of existence.
  2. Respect the sacred afternoon nap.
  3. Do not disturb another creature's peace.
  4. Embrace curiosity without demanding certainty.
  5. Remember that being small does not mean being insignificant.

The Sacred Book of Leaves

These fictional verses form the spiritual literature of Quokkism.

Leaf I — On the Abyss

“We gazed into the abyss. The abyss gazed back. We offered it a leaf.”

Leaf II — On Ambition

“Not every creature must conquer the world. Some are called to sit beneath a tree.”

Leaf III — On Uncertainty

“When you do not know the way, be curious. When you are tired, rest.”

Daily Rituals

The Daily Smile: Notice one small good thing. The Sacred Nap: Rest without apology. The Gathering of Quokkas: Share stories and harmless nonsense with friends.
