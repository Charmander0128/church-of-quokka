🐾 The Church of Quokka

Smile into the Abyss.

The universe owes us nothing. We shall smile anyway.

The Church of Quokka is a fictional, satirical philosophy inspired by the smiling Australian quokka. Its imaginary teachings celebrate kindness, curiosity, simple pleasures and the sacred afternoon nap.

The Five Commandments

  1. Smile at the absurdity of existence.
  2. Respect the sacred afternoon nap.
  3. Do not disturb another creature's peace.
  4. Embrace curiosity without demanding certainty.
  5. Remember that being small does not mean being insignificant.

The Sacred Book of Leaves

We gazed into the abyss. The abyss gazed back. We offered it a leaf.

The Holy Council of Quokkas

In fictional Church mythology, the Holy Council protects the Daily Smile, the Sacred Leaf and the Eternal Nap.

This is a fictional creative project, not a real religious institution or a Wikipedia article. Respect wild quokkas: do not feed or handle them.
