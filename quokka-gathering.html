The Gathering of Quokkas

This is fictional worldbuilding and satire, not an account of a real religious institution.

The Gathering of Quokkas is an imaginary social tradition within the Church of Quokka.

In-universe background

Participants exchange amusing stories and philosophical puzzles, observe a moment of quiet, and celebrate the ordinary joys of friendship.
