Quokkism

This article describes a fictional, satirical creation, not a real religion or historical figure.

Quokkism: Quokkism is the fictional philosophical tradition of the Church of Quokka. It celebrates existential optimism, kindness, curiosity and the sacred afternoon nap.

In-universe background

Its Five Commandments encourage smiling at absurdity, respecting rest, leaving others in peace, welcoming uncertainty and remembering that small creatures matter.
